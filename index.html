Snap it. Check it. Hand it in.

Three steps from a crumpled slip to something you can submit.

  1. Snap it

    Use your camera, or drop in a PDF, JPG or PNG up to 10 MB. Add a few files at once.

  2. Check it

    Stash suggests a name, reference number, amount, date and category. Fix anything that’s off, then save.

  3. Hand it in

    Build a clearance summary PDF from the documents you pick, or give a cafe a PIN for a single document.

Scan

Stash reads the slip, so you don’t retype it.

Take a photo of a receipt or drop in a PDF. Stash picks out the reference number, amount and date, suggests a name and category, and fills in the level and semester when it can find them.

You always get the last word: every detail stays editable before you save.
